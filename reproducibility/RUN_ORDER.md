# Frozen manuscript workflow order

Always write to a new output root. Never point these workflows at the archived manuscript results.

## 1. Present-state zebrafish adaptation

1. audit provider files and coordinates;
2. freeze protocol and source-only anchor panels;
3. fit source models and geometry-only registrations;
4. extract target anchors only;
5. serialize and hash predictions;
6. open hidden target genes for evaluation;
7. produce frozen summaries and source panels.

## 2. External comparators

Run gimVI and TransImpSpa under their recorded information settings. Keep the comparison universe fixed and do not use their outcomes to retune HyperSpatial-MAP.

## 3. Residual-biology audits

These analyses consume frozen residual fields. Negative-binomial null, annotation association, registration robustness, and source-only panel-budget analyses do not refit HyperSpatial-MAP.

## 4. MAP-T forecasting

Use the frozen reciprocal E1/E2 folds. Later target expression remains withheld until transition predictions are locked.

## 5. External 2D DLPFC

For every reciprocal section direction: source-only fit → target-anchor extraction → prediction → global seal → evaluation. Sections and donors retain their recorded statistical roles.

## 6. SPATCH cross-platform transfer

Use geometry-only Visium HD registration, exactly 16 exposed target genes, and registered 12-NN IDW as the manuscript-facing comparator. Xenium and CosMx branches retain their separate frozen outputs.

## 7. Stroke and liver disease

Run stroke, MASLD, PSC, and alcohol-associated hepatitis only under their frozen configurations. The liver protocol was frozen after MASLD and then applied unchanged to PSC and AH. Disease programs and representative candidates remain predeclared/source-selected.

## 8. Plot source panels

Only after frozen-table verification, run serialized panel renderers. Do not assemble the final manuscript layouts in this repository.
