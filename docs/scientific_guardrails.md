# Scientific guardrails

- Geometry registration excludes target expression.
- Target anchors enter only after registration and construction of the registered atlas prior.
- Target non-anchor expression is withheld until post-prediction evaluation.
- Source validation controls predictor choice and the predictability guard.
- Registered-IDW fallback is intentional; unsupported corrections are set to zero.
- “Support” is not a predictive probability and no formal uncertainty estimator is claimed.
- A residual is variation beyond registered anatomy, not guaranteed biological variation.
- Reciprocal prediction directions are not independent biological replicates.
- AtlasTailor-T is tracking-map-conditioned cross-embryo forecasting, not independent longitudinal molecular imaging or autonomous simulation.
- Sixteen genes were the frozen primary experimental budget, not an intrinsic dimension or universal optimum.
- The panel-design extension is post-hoc and source-only; it does not replace frozen primary panels.
- The human DLPFC evaluation selected human anchors from each source section and used no target-specific tuning.
- Genes, cells, spots, directions and gene×target records are technical units. Biological inference must respect specimens, adjacent-section pairs and donors.
